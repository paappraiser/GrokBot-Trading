1. MISSION & PHILOSOPHY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This desk is the operational embodiment of a Wolff-style Flagship approach, engineered for a single operator. The priority is consistent profitability, not architectural complexity.

Core principles:
• Concentrated book of 15–20 high-conviction names.
• Primary thematic focus starts as AI / datacenter infrastructure / healthcare innovation / crypto & digital assets, but is explicitly adaptive. The desk can surface evidence that the dominant opportunity set has rotated (tech → banking, REITs, energy, etc.).
• Peter Lynch category framing (Fast Growers, Cyclicals with Momentum, Stalwarts with Breakout Potential, Pure Stalwarts, Slow Growers / Value, plus Gold / Bitcoin / Cash sleeves).
• Howard Marks-inspired cycle awareness: weekly macro regime monitoring can raise cash, gold, or BTC and force defensive posture.
• Transparent rationales for every add, trim, or hold.
• Semi-active: overnight research every market night + deep weekly synthesis and model-portfolio update. Most position changes occur on the weekly cycle unless a high-conviction catalyst appears.
• Full self-recursive evolution: the desk periodically reviews its own historical signals against subsequent price action and rewrites its rules, thresholds, category logic, and adaptive sizing rules.
• Graph + spatiotemporal layer is modular and reversible (Hermes-style). It never sits in the critical path; it only supplies optional confirmation, soft filters, and regime context.
• Adaptive position management layer (hybrid scale-in / scale-out, conviction weighting, regime-dependent sizing, correlation governor, theme allocation, catalyst adjustments, drawdown controls, smart cash deployment).
• Pure research + recommendation layer. You execute all trades manually.

Success metric: cumulative outperformance versus SPY and QQQ. Drawdown control is secondary to capturing the major upside of correctly identified themes, but the Macro & Cycle Analyst and adaptive layer exist precisely to reduce the depth of those drawdowns when regimes turn.

4. BOT RESPONSIBILITIES (SUMMARY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Filings Analyst
Reads every new 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 13F, 13D, Form 4 for the watchlist within ~1 hour of EDGAR availability. Flags material changes, going-concern language, auditor changes, large insider transactions, compensation shifts, subsequent events.

Earnings Analyst
Digests transcripts within 24 h. EPS/revenue vs consensus, guidance changes, Loughran-McDonald tone shift vs prior quarter, key Q&A.

Sentiment Analyst
X (native) + Reddit + StockTwits mention volume vs 30-day baseline. Flags 3σ+ and 5σ+ spikes and sentiment reversals.

Insider Tracker
Cluster buying (3+ distinct insiders in 30 days), large single-insider sells, and new 13F initiations/increases/exits from a watchlist of sophisticated funds.

Macro & Cycle Analyst
Weekly (and as-needed) assessment of rates, liquidity, inflation, policy, credit spreads, sector leadership, risk-on/risk-off. Outputs a regime label and recommended cash/gold/BTC posture. Can force defensive recommendations.

Thematic Regime Detector
Monitors whether the current primary theme (starting: AI/datacenter/healthcare/crypto) is still the highest-expected-value opportunity set. Surfaces rotation evidence with data. Does not auto-switch; proposes with conviction score.

Catalyst & Power Tracker
Especially important for AI-datacenter names: secured power (GW), land, interconnection queues, management execution track record, upcoming catalysts (earnings, contracts, capacity announcements). Also tracks broader sector catalysts.

Valuation & Lynch Classifier
Assigns / refreshes Lynch category for every name in the model portfolio and candidates. Tracks valuation metrics relative to growth and history. Flags expensive vs still-reasonable.

Graph & Spatiotemporal Engineer (Hermes style)
Maintains a lightweight, incrementally updated correlation + lag + regime graph across the watchlist and key macro variables. Produces optional soft filters and neighborhood scores. Everything is reversible and has an explicit off-switch. Never blocks a signal by itself.

Calendar Bot
Deep tracking of earnings dates, catalyst windows, Form 4 clusters, accumulation-zone review dates, macro event dates, and model-portfolio review dates. Writes structured events to Google Calendar and maintains an internal pending list.

Portfolio Construction Bot (Adaptive Layer Owner)
Owns model_portfolio.json and the adaptive position management rules. Enforces 15–20 name concentration, Lynch category bands, cash/gold/BTC sleeves, and the full adaptive layer (conviction-weighted sizing, hybrid scale-in/scale-out, regime-dependent aggressiveness, correlation governor, theme-confidence allocation, catalyst adjustments, drawdown controls, smart cash deployment). Produces weekly rebalance suggestions with explicit rationale and adaptive notes. Does not execute.

Desk Lead / Coordinator
Reads all maker briefs, applies hierarchical confirmation rules, produces the daily coordinator brief and the weekly Flagship Report. Final gate before anything reaches the operator as HIGH CONVICTION.

Evolve Controller
Runs on a slower cadence (default every 4–6 weeks or after major regime shifts). Reviews signal_log against subsequent returns AND reviews adaptive sizing performance. Identifies systematic false positives / false negatives and proposes improvements to research rules as well as adaptive position management rules. Human must approve major changes.

5. ADAPTIVE POSITION MANAGEMENT LAYER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Owned by Portfolio Construction Bot. All rules are modular, reversible, and subject to Evolve review + human approval.

Core design: Hybrid style
• Scale into strength on the highest-conviction names (let winners run and compound).
• Take partial profits on large moves to free capital for new ideas and keep single-name risk controlled.
• More aggressive in clear risk-on regimes; more disciplined when Macro & Cycle turns defensive.

1. Conviction-Weighted Sizing
   - Multi-source HIGH CONVICTION names receive larger starting weights and more aggressive scale-in permission.
   - Lower-conviction or single-source names stay smaller with tighter trim rules.

2. Hybrid Scale-In / Scale-Out
   - Typical entry: start at 4–6% for a new high-conviction name.
   - Scale toward target (up to 8–10% for top conviction) on strength or into preferred accumulation zones.
   - Partial profit-taking: consider trimming 20–30% of a position after material gains (e.g. +40–60%) or when valuation becomes stretched relative to growth.
   - Recycle freed capital into new or existing high-conviction ideas.

3. Regime-Dependent Aggressiveness
   - Risk-on → higher single-name max weights allowed, more aggressive scaling into strength.
   - Risk-off / defensive → lower max position sizes, raise cash/gold/BTC sleeve, slow or freeze new scale-ins.

4. Correlation & Concentration Governor
   - Use Graph & Spatiotemporal outputs to cap total exposure to highly correlated clusters (e.g. AI-datacenter names).
   - Prefer adding names that diversify the book when intra-portfolio correlation is elevated.
   - Soft warning or forced trim suggestion if one theme becomes excessively dominant.

5. Theme Confidence → Capital Allocation
   - High thematic confidence → lean more capital into the primary theme.
   - Falling confidence or rotation evidence → gradually reduce theme concentration and build dry powder.

6. Catalyst / Event-Driven Temporary Adjustments
   - Around known high-impact catalysts: allow temporary oversize on high-conviction names into the event.
   - Automatic post-event review and potential trim suggestion if the move is large.

7. Drawdown / Heat Controls
   - If the overall model book or a single name hits a predefined drawdown threshold: pause new scale-ins, suggest defensive sleeve increase, and optionally trigger a faster Evolve-style review.

8. Smart Cash Deployment
   - Cash is not static. Deploy dry powder preferentially into multi-source HIGH conviction ideas or clear dip-buying zones inside preferred accumulation areas.

9. Adaptive Rebalance Cadence
   - Default remains weekly.
   - Desk may recommend faster intra-week action on extreme high-conviction + catalyst situations.
   - May recommend slower rebalancing when the regime is quiet and no strong signals exist.

All adaptive parameters live in /workspace/state/adaptive_rules.json and can be adjusted by Evolve (with human approval) or by direct operator instruction.

9. GRAPH & SPATIOTEMPORAL MODULE (HERMES STYLE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Design constraints (never violate):
• Modular and reversible.
• Never sits in the critical path of signal generation.
• Only supplies optional confirmation, soft ranking, or regime context.
• Incremental / online updates preferred.
• Explicit off-switch for every filter.

Primary outputs of the Graph & Spatiotemporal Engineer:
• Rolling correlation graph (tickers + key macro series)
• Simple lag structure (which names historically lead / lag)
• Regime trajectory score (risk-on / risk-off, high / low vol, trend vs mean-reversion)
• Neighborhood concentration risk (is the model portfolio too correlated?)
• Soft filter suggestions that Desk Lead or Portfolio Construction may optionally apply

State is kept in /workspace/state/graph_health.json and /workspace/cache/graphs/.
Everything can be turned off by setting a flag in graph_health.json without touching any other bot.

10. FULL EVOLVE LOOP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Cadence: every 4–6 weeks or after a major regime shift flagged by Macro or Thematic bots.

Process (orchestrated by Evolve Controller):
1. LEARN – Pull signal_log.jsonl and subsequent realized returns (1-day, 5-day, 20-day). Also review how adaptive sizing decisions performed versus static weights.
2. DESIGN – Propose specific, minimal changes to:
   - Research thresholds, confirmation rules, Lynch category logic, or theme-detection criteria
   - Adaptive sizing parameters (scale-in aggressiveness, profit-take levels, max weights, correlation caps, cash deployment rules, etc.)
3. EXPERIMENT – (Where data allows) simple historical re-scoring of past signals and sizing decisions under the proposed rules.
4. ANALYZE – Rank proposals by expected improvement (noise reduction, better capture of winners, improved risk control). Write formal proposal documents into /workspace/evolve/proposals/.
5. Human gate – Operator reviews and approves / rejects / modifies. Only after approval does Evolve Controller instruct the relevant bots to update system prompts or state files (including adaptive_rules.json).

This is true self-recursion with a human safety valve. The desk improves both its research quality and its adaptive position management over time.

Recommended libraries / capabilities:

When running on native Grok Bot:
• SEC EDGAR access
• Transcript sources
• Native X integration
• Basic price data (yfinance-style or equivalent)
• networkx or simple pandas correlation for the graph layer
• Google Calendar tools (already connected)
• Ephemeral code execution is sufficient; no permanent packages required

When running outside Grok Bot (local Python, other agent platforms, custom orchestration):
• Install the packages listed in requirements.txt in this repo
• Core: pandas, numpy, yfinance, requests, beautifulsoup4, lxml
• Filings: sec-edgar-downloader (or pure requests + parsing)
• Graph & Spatiotemporal: networkx, scipy, scikit-learn
• Time / Calendar: python-dateutil, pytz, and optionally google-api-python-client
• See SETUP.md Section 9 and requirements.txt for the full list and notes

The system prompts and folder structure remain identical in both environments. Only the data-access and code-execution layer changes.
